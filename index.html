Privacy first: This is a prototype. Your pasted text is processed entirely in this browser. Nothing is sent to or stored on our backend servers. The page does not call an AI provider or external API.

04 / What this estimate means

This prototype estimates token volume from pasted text using a simple character-based heuristic. It cannot recover hidden system prompts, model-specific tokenization, image/audio/video processing, cached tokens, hardware utilization, data-centre location or real-time energy use. Actual emissions depend on the model, infrastructure, workload and electricity mix. Results are directional scenarios—not audited carbon accounting.
